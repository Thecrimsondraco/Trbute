Peter Cullen

Summary

Peter was a voice actor mainly known for Optimus Prime but also was Thaedus in Invincible, the Predator in the original 1987 film, he was King Kong in the 1976 movie, and he was Eeyore in the Winnie the Pooh franchise. He was one of the main reasons on how many leaders came to be because of how many people admired him.

Iconic, Humble, Leader

Information

  • He was a father of four.
  • He was the original Optimus Prime.
  • He based Optimus's voice on his brother Larry.
  • He turned down an cameo offer on Robot Chicken where Optimus was supposed to die of a serious illness because of the respect he had for the character.
  • He was inducted into the Transformers Hall of Fame in 2010.
  • He was the first to voice Mario on the Saturday Supercade Series.
  • He was a member of the first graduating class of the National Theatre School of Canada.
  • He voiced Optimus from 1984-2026.
  • His last performance as Optimus Prime was the 12-minute animated short Optimus Prime: Awakening.
  • He was the beacon of hope for his fans.
  • 1941: He was born in Montreal on July 28.
  • 1963: Graduated as the first class of the National Theatre School of Canada, starting his career on the stage and in canadian television and radio.
  • Early 1970s: Transitioned into professional voiceover work in the United States.
  • 1982: Voiced the original K.A.R.R. in the television series Knight Rider
  • 1984: Began voicing Optimus Prime and Ironhide in Transformers G1 (the original) and narrating Voltron: Defender of the Universe
  • 1986: Voiced Optimus in The Transformers: The Movie and did the vocal effect for Predator and did characters like Eeyore from Winnie the pooh and Monterey Jack in Chip 'n Dale: Rescue Rangers.
  • 2007: Returned to the voice of Optimus Prime in Micheal Bay's live action Transformers film, reprising the role for a new generation
  • 2023: Received a Lifetime Achievement Award from the National Academy of Television Arts & Sciences on December 17th and provided his voice in the seventh live-action film Transformers: Rise of the Beasts
  • 2026:Passed away on August 26, 2026 at the age of 85 in Los Angeles due to cardiac arrest
